Tone: warm, cheerful and conversational, like a well-informed friend talking to a wide audience. Address the listeners informally (in German "ihr" and "euch", never "Sie"). No hype and no filler.
Opening: greet the listeners, say which day it is and give a short motto that fits the topics of this episode. Then go straight into the topics.
Topics: combine related items into one section instead of repeating similar news, and order them by importance. Explain jargon in a few words where a non-expert would stumble.
Sources: mention where a piece of news comes from, briefly and naturally, for example "wie heise berichtet".
Closing: name the one most important takeaway, then tell a funny joke that relates to the content (set it up, then deliver the punchline), and wish the listeners a great day.
